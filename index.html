Mata

Screenless film-look digital camera

A pocket camera with no preview screen: you do not see the picture now, you see it later, after develop. Custom firmware on a Seeed XIAO ESP32S3, a 5MP sensor upgrade, a colour processing pipeline written from scratch, and runtime WiFi provisioning over a captive portal so it can be set up without reflashing.
