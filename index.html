Mata Cam

Screenless film-look digital camera

A "screenless camera" inspired by retro-styled digital "film" cameras. Custom firmware on a Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, with an image processing pipeline towards a film look. Custom-designed 3D-printed enclosure to suit the board, function, and aesthetic. WiFi-based setup for gallery and offloading.

  • C++
  • ESP32-S3
  • PlatformIO
  • Image pipeline
  • Blender
  • 3D printing
